## Year by year

| Year | Dominant style | Started | Peak | What it reacted to | Became | Runner-ups that year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | [Material Design](eras/2016-material-design.md) | 2014-06 | 2015–2016 | Flat design's missing affordances | Material 2 → Material You | Brutalism (press), card UIs, hamburger menus |
| 2017 | [Web Brutalism](eras/2017-brutalism.md) | 2014 | 2016–2017 | Bootstrap and Material sameness | Neubrutalism | Duotone, bold gradients, split screens |
| 2018 | [Vibrant Gradients & Flat 2.0](eras/2018-gradients-flat-2.md) | 2016 | 2018 | Flat design's sterility | Mesh gradients, glass backgrounds | Isometric illustration, Corporate Memphis, asymmetric layouts |
| 2019 | [Dark Mode](eras/2019-dark-mode.md) | 2018-09 | 2019–2020 | Bright screens everywhere, OLED | A baseline expectation | Big bold type, 3D illustration, micro-interactions |
| 2020 | [Neumorphism](eras/2020-neumorphism.md) | 2019-12 | 2020 | Flat's lack of depth | Claymorphism, then abandoned | 3D illustration, gradients, dark mode |
| 2021 | [Glassmorphism](eras/2021-glassmorphism.md) **(deep)** | 2020-11 | 2021 | Neumorphism's colourless low contrast | Linear glows, Liquid Glass | Claymorphism, 3D blobs, Y2K revival |
| 2022 | [Neubrutalism](eras/2022-neubrutalism.md) | 2021-11 | 2022–2023 | Glossy corporate SaaS | Mainstream "playful" kits | Y2K, variable fonts, kinetic type |
| 2023 | [Linear Style](eras/2023-linear-style.md) | 2021 | 2023–2024 | Bright, illustrated SaaS pages | Saturated "every SaaS looks the same" | Bento, AI gradient orbs, grain textures |
| 2024 | [Bento Grid](eras/2024-bento-grid.md) | 2022 | 2023–2024 | Long alternating feature rows | A standard section pattern | Swiss and editorial revival, scroll-driven animation |
| 2025 | [Liquid Glass](eras/2025-liquid-glass.md) | 2025-06 | 2025–2026 | Flat iOS 7-era UI, 12 years old | (in progress) | Tactile or "anti-AI" handmade looks, editorial serif revival |

## 2026: watch list (not yet an era)

As of late 2026 no single style has clearly taken over. Candidates seen in roundups (tentative, opinion sources):

- **Liquid Glass echoes on the web:** floating glass nav pills and translucent toolbars.
- **"Barely-there" minimalism:** less chrome, more type and whitespace.
- **Tactile / bold brutalism:** a handmade, imperfect counter-trend to AI-generated polish.

Contributions welcome once one of these clearly wins. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
